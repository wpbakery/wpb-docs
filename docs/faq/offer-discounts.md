# Does WPBakery Page Builder offer discounts?

Yes, we do!

We offer bulk discounts, which can be checked [here](/docs/docs/licensing/agency-program).

To get a student discount, please contact us at support@wpbakery.com from your University-assigned email.

WPBakery also rewards long-term Support Plus subscribers with increasing discounts. Each year you renew, you save an additional 5% on the renewal fee up to a maximum of 50% off. The discount is applied automatically as long as your Support Plus subscription remains active. You can find more about our Loyalty Program [here](/docs/docs/licensing/support-plus/#loyalty-program).
