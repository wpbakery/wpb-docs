# Who is applicable for support at support.wpbakery.com?

WPBakery support is available for those who obtained a regular, 5 sites, 10 sites, or extended license of WPBakery Page Builder on wpbakery.com ([get your license](http://wpbakery.com)) for one year with the option to renew manually.

There is also an option to purchase [Support Plus](/docs/docs/licensing/support-plus) subscription at checkout. Support Plus offers additional features such as priority tickets, our access to our Loyalty Program, and Templatera and Easy Tables free of charge. 

For those who received WPBakery Page Builder bundled with a theme – support is provided by your theme author (because they license end product to you). If you purchased WPBakery Page Builder within a theme you can still access WPBakery Support Portal – [support.wpbakery.com](http://support.wpbakery.com/) – to use support guide available under the Support section.

## What benefits our users receive?

- Direct, automatic updates of the latest WPBakery Page Builder versions
- Access to official WPBakery – WPBakery Page Builder support at [support.wpbakery.com](http://support.wpbakery.com/)
- Ability to use WPBakery Page Builder with any WP theme (Note: not just the one that includes WPBakery Page Builder inbuilt version)
- With the [WPBakery Loyalty Program](/docs/docs/licensing/support-plus/#loyalty-program), subscription renewals automatically unlock increasing discounts year after year

**Important:** Automatic updates and access to support are only available with an [active support](/docs/docs/licensing/support-plus) tenure.
