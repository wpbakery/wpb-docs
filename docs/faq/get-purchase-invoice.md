# Where can I get my purchase invoice?

After your purchase, an invoice will be sent to the email address provided during checkout.

If for any reason you did not receive the invoice or need a copy to be re-sent, please open a ticket via our support portal at [support.wpbakery.com](https://support.wpbakery.com/tickets) and our team will be happy to assist you with obtaining a copy of the invoice.
