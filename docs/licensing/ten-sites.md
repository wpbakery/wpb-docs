# 10 Sites

A 10 Sites license allows using the plugin on up to 10 websites. All the websites on which the license is used will receive plugin updates. These licenses can be used on your own websites or on your client's websites.

You can purchase the 10 Sites License from [here](https://wpbakery.com/wpbakery-page-builder-license/) and see the other plans [here](https://wpbakery.com/wpbakery-page-builder-license/).
