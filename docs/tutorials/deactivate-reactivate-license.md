You can deactivate a WPBakery Page Builder license from your WordPress dashboard or from the WPBakery Customer Center.

## Deactivate a license from WordPress

If you still have access to the WordPress website:

1. Go to **WPBakery Page Builder - Product license** in your WordPress dashboard.
2. Click **Deactivate**.

## Deactivate a license from the WPBakery Customer Center

If the website has already been removed or you cannot deactivate the license from WordPress, log in to the [WPBakery Customer Center](https://support.wpbakery.com/) and:

1. Open the **Licenses** section.
2. Find the license and the domain where it is currently activated.
3. Deactivate the license for that domain.

## Reactivate a WPBakery Page Builder license

To activate the license again:

1. In your WordPress dashboard, go to **WPBakery Page Builder - Product license**.
2. Click **Activate WPBakery Page Builder**.
3. Log in to your WPBakery account when redirected to the Customer Center.
4. Select the available license you want to activate.
5. Confirm the activation.

After activation, you will be redirected back to your WordPress website.

## License activation limit

Each WPBakery Page Builder license can be deactivated and activated on up to three unique domains [within a 30-day period](https://kb.wpbakery.com/docs/getting-started/activation/).
