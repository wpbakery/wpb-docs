To apply for a refund for the WPBakery Page Builder within 15 days after a purchase, you will need to contact our support:

1. Log in to the Support Portal – [support.wpbakery.com](https://support.wpbakery.com/tickets) and navigate to the 'Support' tab;
2. Open a support ticket;
3. Provide your license number and explain why you would like a refund;
4. Provide any additional information that the support team may need to process your refund request, such as screenshots of the issue you're experiencing;
5. Submit ticket.
