To reassign your WPBakery page builder license to another site, you will need to follow these steps:

1. Log in to the Support Portal – [support.wpbakery.com](https://support.wpbakery.com/tickets) and navigate to the 'Licenses' tab;
2. Find the license you want to reassign and click on the "Deactivate" button'
3. Once the license has been deactivated, you can now copy the license key;
4. Open the site where you want to reassign the license and under WP Dashboard -> WPBakery Page Builder -> Product License tab you will find the 'Activate' button where you can paste the license key.
