To change your WPBakery Page Builder license credentials, you can:

- Contact the support team at support.wpbakery.com if you need to change the email address associated with your license.
- Go to the password reset page at [support.wpbakery.com](https://support.wpbakery.com/reset-password) and enter the email address associated with your license, then follow the instructions in the email to reset your password.

Please make sure to keep your credentials up to date to ensure access to your license, updates, and support from WPBakery.
